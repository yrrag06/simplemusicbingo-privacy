Simple Music Bingo – Privacy Policy
Last updated: 18 November 2026

Simple Music Bingo does not collect, store or share any personal information.

• Player names, games and bingo cards are stored only on your device. They are shared only when you choose to show or print a card's QR code.
• Apple Music: songs and playlists are provided by Apple through your own Apple Music account. Apple's privacy policy applies.
• Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
• iCloud: the app keeps a small note in your own iCloud account so your free game is remembered across your devices. We cannot see it.
• Camera: used only to scan bingo card QR codes. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.
• There are no adverts, analytics, tracking or accounts.
